Files deposited in CaltechDATA are stored on the Open Storage Network (OSN), a shared storage platform designed for the research community. Caltech Library manages the Caltech OSN pods, which were funded by the National Science Foundation under Grant 2322420. Any opinions, findings, and conclusions or recommendations expressed in CaltechDATA are those of the uploaders and do not necessarily reflect the views of the National Science Foundation.

Storage limits

  • Each record can hold up to 10 GB of files by default.
  • You can allocate up to 1 TB of additional storage to any record by using the "Manage storage" button.
  • A single file can be up to 50 GB.
  • The upload form accepts up to 50 files per record.

If your dataset is larger than these limits, contact us at data@caltech.edu before you upload, and we will help you find the best way to deposit it.
